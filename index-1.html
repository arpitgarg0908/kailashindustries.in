Manufacturing process

Seven stages, from raw material to packed pellets

Our biomass pellets are made at our plant in Bikaner, in seven stages from raw material procurement to packaging.

Production samples are tested for GCV, moisture and ash.

See the test results

  1. Stage 01

    Raw material procurement

    Biomass raw material is procured and brought to our plant in Bikaner.

  2. Stage 02

    Screening

    The raw material is screened to remove unwanted foreign matter before processing.

  3. Stage 03

    Grinding

    Screened material is ground to a uniform size suitable for pelletizing.

  4. Stage 04

    Drying

    Ground material is dried to the moisture level needed for pelletizing.

  5. Stage 05

    Pelletizing

    Dried material is compressed into 6 mm pellets.

  6. Stage 06

    Cooling

    Pellets are cooled before packing, so they can be handled and stored.

  7. Stage 07

    Packaging

    Finished pellets are packed and loaded on trucks for delivery.

Common questions

Before you order

Will these pellets work in my burner?

It depends on your burner design, fuel feeding system and operating needs. We offer 6 mm pellets. Please confirm compatibility with your burner or boiler supplier before ordering, and share your setup with us so we can discuss it.

What do GCV, moisture and ash mean?

GCV (Gross Calorific Value) is the heat one kg of fuel gives when it burns, in kcal/kg. Moisture is the share of water in the fuel, which lowers the usable heat. Ash is the solid left after burning, which has to be cleared from the burner. A higher GCV with lower moisture and ash is generally preferred.

Are the test reports from an independent laboratory?

No. They are our own internal quality-control reports for specific samples. They are not independent third-party certificates. Ask us if your procurement team needs additional documentation.

Do the results apply to every batch?

No. Each report covers one sample on one test date. Ask us for the latest batch report before you make a supply decision.

How should the pellets be stored?

Our reports advise storing the pellets in dry conditions to maintain quality.

Do you deliver across India, and how much per truck?

Yes. We deliver across India by truck, with a minimum of 10 tonnes and up to 40 tonnes per truck. Tell us the quantity and delivery location on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability and terms.
